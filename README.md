# BhumiMitra — National Land Intelligence & Policy Simulation Platform

> A self-contained, trilingual demonstration platform for land intelligence, GIS analysis,
> land-policy simulation, digital land twins, dispute early-warning analytics, research
> intelligence and AI-assisted land-governance research.
